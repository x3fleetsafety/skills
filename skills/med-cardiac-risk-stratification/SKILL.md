# Cardiac Risk Stratification for CMV Certification

Cardiac conditions are evaluated under § 391.41(b)(4)-(7). Medical examiners consult FMCSA Medical Review Board guidelines for specific conditions.

## Conditions that may disqualify (with timing)
- **Acute myocardial infarction:** typically 3 months minimum recovery before recertification
- **Coronary artery bypass graft (CABG):** typically 3 months minimum
- **Percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI / stent):** typically 1 week minimum
- **Implantable cardioverter-defibrillator (ICD):** generally disqualifying for primary prevention; may be eligible for secondary prevention
- **Heart failure:** depends on ejection fraction; under 40% typically disqualifying without specialist clearance
- **Severe arrhythmias:** AFib, sustained ventricular tachycardia — depends on management
- **Hypertension uncontrolled:** above 180/110 typically disqualifying; controlled to under 140/90 acceptable

## Documentation required for certification post-event
- Cardiologist clearance letter (treating physician)
- Recent ejection fraction (echocardiogram or angiogram)
- Stress test results
- Medication list with stability documentation
- Symptom-free period demonstration

## Continued monitoring
- Annual exam minimum
- Some conditions trigger 6-month exam cycle
- Cardiologist re-evaluation annually for stable conditions; every 6 months for active management

## Common driver concerns
- "I had a stent — can I keep driving?" → after waiting period + cardiologist clearance + medical exam, generally yes
- "I'm on blood thinners — does that disqualify?" → blood thinners alone do NOT disqualify; the underlying condition matters
- "I have an ICD — am I done?" → for primary prevention, likely yes; for secondary prevention with stability + clearance, possibly continuation

## Compass tracking
- Driver's cardiac history flagged if disclosed
- Next medical exam (typically 12 months, sometimes 6)
- Cardiologist visit dates if applicable
- Medication compliance reminders for cardiac drugs

## CFR citations
- **49 CFR § 391.41(b)(4)-(7)**
- **FMCSA Medical Review Board Cardiac Guidelines**
