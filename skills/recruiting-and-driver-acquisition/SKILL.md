# Recruiting & Driver Acquisition

The trucking industry needs 80,000+ drivers (US). Recruiting is the lifeblood of fleets. This skill covers what's working now to source + sign drivers.

## Sourcing channels

### Established channels

| Channel | Reach | Quality | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CDL Schools** | Medium | Variable (entry-level) | $0-$5K per hire |
| **Career.com / Indeed** | High | Mixed | $50-$500 per hire (ads + screening) |
| **DAT Loadboard recruiting** | Trucking-specific | Owner-operator focus | $200/month subscription |
| **Hiring referral bonuses** | Targeted | High (drivers refer good drivers) | $500-$5K per successful hire |
| **Carrier ambassador program** | Network effect | Very high | Modest investment + recognition |
| **Veteran transition programs** | Quality (military discipline) | Strong | $0-$2K per hire |
| **Returning citizens (post-incarceration)** | Specialized | Mixed | $500-$3K per hire |
| **Owner-operator recruitment** | Existing drivers | High experience | $1K-$10K signing bonus |
| **Lease-purchase programs** | New driver entry | Variable | Low; deductions cover |

### Newer channels

| Channel | Reach | Quality | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TikTok / Instagram recruitment** | Younger workforce | Mixed; lots of "what's it like" content | Modest investment |
| **YouTube vlog recruitment** | Story-driven | Strong personal connection | Modest |
| **Apprenticeship programs (RAP)** | New driver pipeline | High | $0-$15K via CMV-DAP grant |
| **Direct text/SMS campaigns** | Re-engaging former drivers | Targeted | Modest |
| **Carrier ambassador on LinkedIn** | Professional network | Premium | Time investment |

## The recruitment funnel

```
Application Submitted    -- ~500/month for a fleet of 50 trucks targeting 5-10 hires
        ↓
First Contact            -- ~250 respond
        ↓
Pre-Screen Call          -- ~150 actually engage
        ↓
DQF Evaluation           -- ~100 pass initial review
        ↓
Drug Test + Clearinghouse -- ~80 pass
        ↓
Job Offer Made           -- ~60 (some withdraw, decline)
        ↓
Accept Offer             -- ~25-30 actually start
        ↓
Survive First 90 Days    -- ~20 (some leave during onboarding)
```

The funnel is wide-bottom because retention compounds over time. A fleet recruiting 10 drivers/month at industry-standard retention is replacing the same 10 drivers who quit.

## Application + screening process

### Standard pre-hire requirements

- Application form (49 CFR 391.21 compliant)
- 3-year employment verification (39 CFR 391.23)
- 3-year MVR (49 CFR 391.25)
- Clearinghouse Full query (49 CFR 382.701)
- Drug test pre-employment (49 CFR 382.301)
- Medical certificate (49 CFR 391.43)
- Background check (FCRA-compliant)
- PSP report (Pre-Employment Screening Program)
- CDL verification
- Telephone reference check
- In-person interview

Many carriers use a vendor (Checkr, HireRight, DriversFacts) to streamline.

## Signing bonuses

| Driver type | Signing Bonus Typical |
|---|---|
| Entry-level company driver | $1K-$3K |
| Experienced regional driver | $3K-$8K |
| OTR experienced driver | $5K-$15K |
| Owner-operator (lease-purchase) | $2K-$10K |
| Owner-operator (own truck) | $1K-$5K |

Paid: 50% at hire, 25% at 90 days, 25% at 1 year (typical structure). Some pay all upfront with clawback if leave early.

## Retention bonuses

| Milestone | Amount |
|---|---|
| 90 days | $500-$1,500 |
| 6 months | $1,000-$3,000 |
| 1 year | $2,000-$5,000 |
| 2 years | $2,500-$5,000 (annual) |
| 5 years | $5,000+ + extra PTO |

Lifetime cost is significant but tracks against turnover cost. A driver staying 5 years vs leaving in 90 days = saved $10K-$30K in re-hire cost.

## Recruitment marketing

Different approaches for different audiences:

### For experienced drivers
- Specific role + lane info
- Comp clearly stated ($/mile + bonus)
- Truck specs (new, well-maintained, with creature comforts)
- Home time clearly stated
- Driver-to-driver testimonials

### For entry-level
- Training program structure
- Cost of training (and how to offset)
- Apprenticeship status (RAP = funding)
- Mentorship + support
- Career path beyond entry

### For owner-operators
- Settlement statement examples (real numbers)
- Lease-purchase terms (transparent)
- Network + load board access
- Insurance support
- Maintenance support

## The Driver Ambassador Program

A driver ambassador is an existing driver who actively recruits new drivers from their network:

- Receives bonus per successful hire (e.g., $1K-$3K)
- Featured in marketing
- Has recognition + status
- Acts as cultural representative

Powerful because:
- Network effect (drivers know drivers)
- Trust-based ("Joe says it's a good place to work")
- Low cost per hire
- Drives retention (drivers feel ownership)

## Veteran recruitment

The trucking-veteran pipeline is strong:
- **Hiring Our Heroes** — DOL program
- **Drive4Heroes** — DOT-funded
- **Trucking industry veterans network** — connects veterans with carriers
- **GI Bill + Post-9/11 GI Bill** — funds CDL training for veterans

Veteran benefits for carriers:
- Strong work ethic + discipline
- Public-relations win
- Tax credits (Work Opportunity Tax Credit — WOTC)

## Returning citizens (post-incarceration)

A growing channel:
- ~600,000 people released from US prisons annually
- Many qualify for CDL training (no major felony in last 5 years for some carriers, varies)
- Higher retention in many cases (motivation to rebuild)
- Federal Workforce Tax Credit available

Considerations:
- Carrier policy on conviction history (carriers vary widely)
- Customer reactions (some don't want returning citizens hauling their freight)
- State + federal restrictions on driver licenses post-conviction

## Common recruitment mistakes

1. **Promising more than delivered.** $0.55/mile in recruiting, $0.45 in reality. Driver quits in 90 days.
2. **Hidden charges in lease-purchase.** Drivers pay $200K for $150K truck.
3. **Slow application response.** Driver applies, hears nothing for 5 days, takes another job.
4. **No CDL school partnership.** Pool of new drivers without training program access.
5. **All recruitment focused on entry-level.** Missing the experienced driver market.
6. **No driver ambassador program.** Existing drivers don't help recruit.
7. **One-size-fits-all approach.** Owner-operators want different things than company drivers.
8. **Recruiter compensation not aligned.** Pay-per-hire only = lots of bad hires.
