# Mock Audit Self-Assessment

Mock audits identify compliance gaps before FMCSA visits. Recommended annually + before any expected official audit.

## Mock audit scope
- Same areas as Compliance Review
- Driver qualification files
- Hours of Service records
- Vehicle maintenance records
- D&A testing records
- Operating authority + insurance
- Accident register

## Step-by-step process
1. **Identify scope**: which carrier areas to audit
2. **Pull sample**: representative selection of drivers, vehicles, trips
3. **Review against regulation**: § by § comparison
4. **Document findings**: what's compliant, what's not
5. **Prioritize gaps**: critical vs minor
6. **Remediate**: address each gap
7. **Re-audit**: confirm remediation
8. **Report**: written summary for carrier management

## Common findings (industry-wide)
- DQ files: missing 1-3 documents per driver
- HOS: occasional 14-hour violations
- Vehicle: maintenance schedule not always followed
- D&A: random rate slightly below target
- Accident register: some events not added
- Insurance: lapsed (not current)

## Tools needed
- Document checklist (per regulation)
- Sample selection method (random)
- Reporting template
- Remediation tracker

## Internal vs external mock audit
- **Internal**: cheaper, faster, less independent
- **External (consultant)**: more expensive, more objective
- **Hybrid**: internal + occasional external for second opinion

## Best practice
Run mock audit:
- Annually (at minimum)
- Before expected FMCSA visit
- After major operational change (new authority, new state, new carrier acquired)
- After significant CSA score change
