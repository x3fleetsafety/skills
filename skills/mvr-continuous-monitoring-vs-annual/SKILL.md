# Continuous MVR Monitoring vs Annual Pull

Annual MVR pulls under § 391.25 satisfy regulation. Continuous monitoring goes further — surfacing violations within hours of conviction. Worth the cost? Depends on fleet size + risk profile.

## Annual MVR (regulatory minimum)
- Pull MVR within 12 months of hire/last review
- Cost: $3-25 per pull (state-dependent)
- Annual driver's certificate of violations + MVR review documents compliance

## Continuous monitoring
- Daily check against state MVR database
- Alert sent within hours of conviction posting
- Cost: $4.95-9/driver/month (SambaSafety, Foley, others)
- Annual cost: ~$60-108/driver

## When continuous monitoring pays off
- Fleets with safety incentives (driver-of-the-year, performance bonuses)
- High-CSA carrier — need violation alerts to be reactive
- Hazmat or high-risk operations
- Insurance carrier discount for continuous monitoring (some insurers offer)
- Driver shortage market — quick intervention prevents losses

## When annual is sufficient
- Smaller fleets where personal knowledge of drivers
- Strong driver-of-the-year discipline (drivers self-report)
- Low-margin operations where the monthly cost doesn't pay off

## Major continuous monitoring providers
- **SambaSafety** — largest, full state coverage, real-time alerts
- **Foley Carrier Services** — bundled MVR + DQF management
- **Driver iQ** — fleet-focused
- **HireRight DAC** — also handles employment + criminal

## How to implement
1. Get driver consent for continuous monitoring (form)
2. Subscribe to provider service
3. Provider monitors state DMV records
4. Alert workflow: SMS/email to safety director
5. Investigation + driver coaching/correction action
