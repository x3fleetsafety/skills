# Used Vehicle Compliance Verification at Purchase

A used vehicle should be inspected and verified compliant before integrating into the fleet. Hidden issues become carrier liability immediately.

## Pre-purchase inspection
- Annual DOT inspection on file (within 12 months)
- Recent service records
- Title clean (no salvage, no flood)
- Mileage verification
- Inspection report from independent mechanic

## Documents to obtain
- Annual inspection certificate
- Service history (oil changes, brake work, major repairs)
- Title (clean)
- VIN check (CARFAX or equivalent for commercial vehicles)
- Prior carrier's DOT number history (to check for major incidents)

## Hidden issues to look for
- Frame damage hidden by paint
- Engine block welding or shoddy repairs
- Brake friction surface excessive wear
- Tire age (DOT date code) regardless of tread
- Suspension wear (especially leaf springs)
- Wheel-end leaks not addressed
- Lighting fixtures recently swapped

## Post-purchase actions
1. Full DOT inspection by your mechanic
2. Brake adjustment check
3. Tire age verification (consider re-tiring soon)
4. Maintenance file setup (start fresh with PM schedule)
5. Lighting + reflector verification
6. ELD installation (if needed)
7. Annual inspection if more than 12 months old

## What NOT to skip
- Verify VIN matches title and registration
- Verify accident history (police record, insurance claim history)
- Verify any FMCSA violations against the vehicle
- Verify previous carrier had no compliance issues
