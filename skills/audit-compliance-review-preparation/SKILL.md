# Compliance Review (CR) Preparation

A Compliance Review (CR) is FMCSA's most comprehensive carrier audit. Outcome can be Satisfactory, Conditional, or Unsatisfactory rating.

## When you receive notice
- Letter from FMCSA Field Office
- Typically 30 days notice
- Reason for review specified (BASIC alerts, accidents, complaint, random)
- Date + location of review

## What's reviewed
- Driver Qualification Files (sample)
- Hours of Service records (sample)
- Vehicle maintenance + inspection records
- Drug & Alcohol records
- Insurance + operating authority
- Accident register + history

## Document production
1. Driver list (all drivers, current + past 12 months)
2. DQ files for sampled drivers
3. RODS for sampled drivers + dates
4. Vehicle inspection records for sampled vehicles
5. Drug + Alcohol testing records
6. Accident register
7. Insurance documentation (MCS-90)
8. Operating authority documents

## Preparation strategy
- **Inventory** all documents required
- **Verify completeness** (no missing items)
- **Address known gaps** (e.g., complete any overdue inspections)
- **Train** the responder (one person handles the review)
- **Coordinate** with safety director, drivers, mechanics

## Day-of conduct
- Provide requested documents promptly
- Don't volunteer information beyond what's asked
- Take notes during the review
- Politely answer questions
- Don't admit fault or guilt

## Common findings
- Missing DQ documents (especially prior employer inquiries, annual MVRs)
- Incomplete HOS records
- Vehicle inspection lapses
- Driver D&A testing not current
- Late or missing accident register entries

## Outcomes
- **Satisfactory**: continue operating
- **Conditional**: must implement corrective action within 60 days
- **Unsatisfactory**: cease operations within 45-60 days

## Appeal process
- Conditional + Unsatisfactory ratings can be appealed
- 30-day window to request reconsideration
- Document corrective actions taken
- Re-review possible
