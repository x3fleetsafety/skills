# Dedicated vs OTR vs Regional vs Local — Comparison

CMV operations are categorized by route + schedule + customer relationship. Understanding the categories helps drivers + carriers match operation type to lifestyle + business goals.

## The four operation types

| Type | Home Daily? | Lane | Customer Relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local** | Yes (always) | Within 50-100 miles of terminal | Often direct shipper |
| **Regional** | 1-3 days/week | Within 500-700 miles of terminal | Mixed (shipper + broker) |
| **OTR** | Once every 2-3 weeks | Nationwide | Mostly broker |
| **Dedicated** | Variable | Specific lanes / customer | Direct shipper contract |

## Local operations

### Description
- Home daily
- Same terminal each day
- Often 5 days on / 2 off (M-F or T-Sat)
- Multiple stops per day or single dedicated route

### Lifestyle
- Most family-friendly
- Predictable schedule
- Limited "career-ending" injury risk
- Often slower-paced driving

### Equipment
- Day cabs (no sleeper berth needed)
- Smaller trucks sometimes (Class B / refrigerated reefer)
- Older equipment OK (limited mileage daily)

### Pay
- Salary: $50K-$70K typical
- Hourly: $20-$30/hr
- Lower per-mile (different metric; ~$0.45 if measured this way)
- Annual: similar to entry OTR; lower than experienced OTR

### Drivers typically prefer when
- Family priority over income
- Older driver
- Limited HOS flexibility (medical conditions, family obligations)
- New driver entering industry

## Regional operations

### Description
- 1-3 days home per week
- 500-700 mile radius
- Often Monday-Friday driving + weekend home
- "Regional reefer" or "regional dry van" common

### Lifestyle
- Better than OTR; less than local
- Predictable enough to plan around
- Some weekend errands possible
- Less family disruption than OTR

### Equipment
- Day cab OR sleeper (depends on lane length)
- Mid-range equipment

### Pay
- $0.55-$0.65/mile typical
- $65K-$95K annually
- Often with weekly mile guarantee

### Drivers typically prefer when
- Married drivers wanting some home time
- Mid-career drivers
- Want stability without local-driver pay ceiling

## OTR (Over-the-Road)

### Description
- 2-3 weeks out, 2-3 days home
- Nationwide
- Often paid by load or per-mile
- Mostly broker-mediated freight

### Lifestyle
- Most demanding
- Limited family/personal life during runs
- Driver lives in cab for weeks
- Most pay but most disruption

### Equipment
- Full sleeper berth
- Often newer equipment (carriers compete for OTR drivers)
- Comfort features (microwave, refrigerator, TV)

### Pay
- $0.60-$0.75/mile
- $70K-$110K annually
- Mile-heavy weeks (3,000-3,500/week typical)

### Drivers typically prefer when
- Single or empty-nesters
- Maximum income priority
- Younger drivers
- Independence value
- Travel-loving lifestyle

## Dedicated

### Description
- Specific contracted lanes for one shipper
- Shipper guarantees volume
- Often returns to same terminal
- More predictable than OTR

### Lifestyle
- More predictable than OTR
- Often Monday-Friday rhythm
- Drivers see same shippers + receivers
- Routine + structure

### Equipment
- Customer-specified sometimes (e.g., shipper logo)
- Newer equipment usually
- Reefer / tanker / specialty if customer requires

### Pay
- $0.55-$0.65/mile
- $65K-$95K annually
- More stable than OTR

### Drivers typically prefer when
- Want OTR-level pay without OTR lifestyle
- Strong customer relationships
- Predictability priority
- Limited interest in spot market

## Choosing the right type for the driver

```
Question: How much home time do you need?
├── Daily → Local
├── Weekends → Regional
├── Every 2-3 weeks → OTR
└── Variable based on lane → Dedicated

Question: How much income do you need?
├── $40K-$60K → Local
├── $60K-$80K → Regional or Dedicated
├── $70K-$110K → OTR
└── Top 10% earner → OTR with team OR specialty owner-operator

Question: How much family disruption are you willing to accept?
├── None → Local
├── Some → Regional
├── A lot → OTR
└── Moderate but predictable → Dedicated

Question: How much equipment freedom do you want?
├── Same truck every day → Dedicated or Local
├── Same truck most of time → OTR (most carriers assign 1 truck per driver)
└── Whatever's available → OTR or Owner-Operator (depending)
```

## Choosing the right type for the carrier

```
Question: What's your customer mix?
├── Few large shippers → Dedicated lanes
├── Mixed shippers + brokers → Mix of OTR + Regional + Dedicated
└── Pure spot market → OTR

Question: What's your equipment investment willing?
├── Older fleet → Local + Regional (less expensive ops)
├── Newer fleet → OTR + Dedicated (premium drivers)
└── Specialty → OTR + Dedicated for specialty customers

Question: What's your driver retention priority?
├── Maximum retention → Local + Dedicated
├── Balance retention + revenue → Regional + Dedicated mix
└── Maximum revenue → OTR (accept higher turnover)
```

## Common hybrid models

### Solo driver, mostly dedicated, some OTR
- Predictable income from dedicated lanes
- Upside from occasional OTR runs
- Used to fill capacity when dedicated below quota

### Team driving on dedicated
- 2 drivers, dedicated lanes
- Maximum miles + maximum pay
- Best of both worlds

### Owner-operator on dedicated
- Independent business with contracted income
- Lower risk than spot market
- Skin in the game
