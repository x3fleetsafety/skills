# Active Driver Record Retention

Different record types have different retention periods. Federal rules are minimums; state law may extend.

## Driver Qualification File
- **Retention**: duration of employment + 3 years after termination
- **What's in DQF**: 12 specific documents per § 391.51

## Hours of Service Records (RODS)
- **Retention**: 6 months from the date of the record
- **Format**: paper or ELD-generated
- **Per § 395.8(k)**

## Vehicle Maintenance + Inspection Records
- **Retention**: 1 year + 6 months after vehicle leaves the carrier's control
- **What's covered**: annual inspections, maintenance logs, DVIRs
- **Per § 396.3(c)**

## Drug & Alcohol Records
- **Negative test results**: 1 year
- **Positive test results, refusals**: 5 years
- **Random selection records**: 2 years
- **Annual MIS reports**: 5 years
- **Per § 382.401**

## Accident Register
- **Retention**: 1 year + 3 years archive (4 years total)
- **What's recorded**: DOT-recordable crashes only
- **Per § 390.15**

## Driver Investigation History (§ 391.23)
- **Retention**: 3 years
- **What's recorded**: prior employer responses, attempts, MVRs

## Other records
- Operating authority documents (MC/DOT): permanent
- Insurance (BMC-91): until replaced
- Process agents (BOC-3): until replaced
- Tax records: 7 years (IRS)
- Payroll records: 7 years (DOL)

## What to keep beyond minimum
- Vehicle maintenance for longer than 1+6 months (insurance, sales)
- Accident records (claim history, subrogation)
- Performance reviews + safety records (HR retention)
