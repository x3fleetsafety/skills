# Brake System Compliance Checks

Brake system is the #1 cited area in roadside inspections. § 393.40 sets construction standards; § 396.3 and § 396.17 set maintenance and inspection.

## What brake checks must occur
1. **Pre-trip** (§ 392.7) — every trip
2. **Post-trip (DVIR)** (§ 396.11) — every duty day
3. **Annual inspection** (§ 396.17) — comprehensive
4. **Periodic inspection** between annuals — carrier's program (typically every 30-90 days)

## Pre-trip brake check
- Air pressure builds + holds (governor cuts off ~120 psi)
- Brakes work in service position
- Brakes work in spring (parking) position
- Brake light functioning
- Air drained from tanks weekly

## DVIR triggers for brakes
- Reduced braking distance
- Pulling to one side
- Squealing or grinding
- Air loss
- Brake light malfunction

## Annual inspection brake requirements
- Each axle brake performance test
- Slack adjuster check (within tolerance per FMCSA Appendix G)
- Brake hose and tubing integrity
- Brake shoe + drum measurements
- Wheel-end inspection for leaks

## Out-of-adjustment rule
A brake is considered "out of service" if more than 20% of brakes on a vehicle are out of adjustment. Compass + maintenance log should track this.

## Common brake violations
- Slack adjuster out of tolerance
- Brake hose contact with another component
- Brake shoe excessive wear
- Air pressure cutoff issues
- Anti-lock brake malfunction lamp on
