# ELDT Behind-The-Wheel Vendor Vetting

Per § 380.609, entry-level driver training (ELDT) for first-time CDL applicants requires both theory (31 topics) and behind-the-wheel (BTW, 19 topics). BTW must be conducted by a TPR-registered provider.

## TPR registry verification
- Go to tpr.fmcsa.dot.gov
- Verify provider's TPR number is active
- Check effective + expiration dates
- Confirm provider has training delivery capability (instructor + vehicles)

## Provider evaluation criteria
- TPR registration current
- Instructor qualifications (some states require additional licensing)
- Training vehicle equipment (proper safety equipment, instructor controls)
- Course materials (FMCSA-approved curriculum)
- Audit history (any FMCSA findings against the provider)
- Reputation (industry references)

## Cost considerations
- BTW typically $200-800 per student
- Theory typically $50-200 per student
- Bundled programs $500-1,200
- Carrier may pay or split cost with driver

## Common pitfalls
- Provider lists 19 BTW topics but actually covers fewer
- Instructor doesn't follow FMCSA-mandated topic order
- Training vehicle doesn't have proper instructor controls
- Provider's TPR registration lapses mid-training (driver records lost)

## What auditors check (if FMCSA reviews the provider)
- Student records (per student)
- Instructor records (qualifications, training delivered)
- Vehicle inspection records (training vehicles)
- Curriculum content
- Time logs (actual training hours)

## Common high-quality TPR providers
- C.R. England Training Schools (large national network)
- Roadmaster Driver School (regional/national)
- Infinit-i (online + partner BTW network)
- Various trucking-company-owned training schools
- Community college CDL programs
