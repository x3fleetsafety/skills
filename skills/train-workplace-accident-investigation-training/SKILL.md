# Workplace Accident Investigation Training

When a crash occurs, the carrier's investigation matters for: insurance claim, FMCSA Crash Preventability Determination Program, lessons learned, regulatory response. Train someone in your fleet to do this.

## Investigation steps
1. **Within 24 hours**: secure documentation (police report, photos, dashcam footage, ELD data, driver statement)
2. **Within 7 days**: interview driver + witnesses
3. **Within 14 days**: technical analysis (vehicle condition, equipment failure investigation)
4. **Within 30 days**: written summary (preventability assessment, lessons learned)

## What to look for
- Police report details
- Citations issued (if any)
- ELD data leading up to crash
- Dashcam footage
- Vehicle inspection of involved unit
- Weather + road conditions
- Driver's HOS status
- Driver's history (prior crashes, violations)
- Mechanical condition of vehicle

## Preventability assessment
- Driver had reasonable opportunity to avoid crash → Preventable
- Driver had limited reasonable opportunity → Possibly preventable
- Driver had no reasonable opportunity → Not preventable

This determination supports the FMCSA Crash Preventability Determination Program filing.

## When to escalate
- Fatal crash → outside expert immediately (insurance, attorney, expert witness)
- Multi-vehicle pile-up → outside expert
- Hazmat release → outside expert + regulatory notification
- Conflicting accounts → outside expert
- Significant property damage → outside expert

## Internal investigator qualification
- Experience handling crashes (not first investigation)
- Understanding of CMV operations
- Familiarity with FMCSA regulations
- Photography + documentation skills
- Interview skills
- Written report writing

## Documentation retention
- All investigation materials retained per FMCSA 1-year + 6 months rule (vehicles + accidents)
- Insurance claim copies retained as long as insurer requires
- Settlement records retained per state statute of limitations
