# Cargo Securement General Rules

Cargo securement rules under § 393 ensure cargo cannot shift, fall, or escape during transport. Violations are common roadside inspection findings.

## Working Load Limit (WLL)
- Each tiedown has a manufacturer-stated WLL
- Total tiedown WLL must equal **at least 50% of cargo weight**
- Some commodities require more (e.g., 80% for steel coils)

## Number of tiedowns required
- 1 tiedown for 5 ft or less of cargo length
- 2 tiedowns for 5-10 ft of length
- Additional tiedown for each additional 10 ft

## Tiedown spacing
- Cargo extending beyond vehicle must be tied at the cargo edge
- Tiedowns must not chafe against the cargo
- Tiedowns must be tightened sufficiently to prevent movement

## Common commodity rules
- **Logs/lumber**: specific to wood + species
- **Steel coils**: dedicated rules with chains + dunnage
- **Heavy machinery**: stronger tiedowns + chocks
- **Pipes**: rigid + secured against rolling
- **Bricks/concrete**: specific to block dimension
- **Light vehicles**: specific to passenger vehicles being hauled
- **Specialized vehicles**: agricultural, military, etc.

## Common violations
- Insufficient tiedowns (lower than required count)
- Tiedowns not tightened
- Tiedown attachment points inadequate
- Tiedowns chafed against cargo
- Cargo extending beyond rated vehicle capacity
