# Samsara vs Motive vs Geotab — ELD/Telematics Comparison

These three vendors dominate the U.S. commercial fleet telematics market. Each is FMCSA-registered as an ELD provider and offers fleet management beyond compliance. Choice affects 5–7 years of operating workflow.

## High-level positioning

| Vendor | Sweet spot | Pricing tier |
|---|---|---|
| Samsara | Mid-to-large fleets, integrated AI dashcam + safety | Premium |
| Motive | Small-to-mid fleets, all-in-one with strong dashcam | Mid |
| Geotab | Mixed fleets, vehicle-data depth, custom integrations | Variable (MarketPlace add-ons) |

## ELD compliance (table stakes — all three pass)

All three are FMCSA-registered ELDs, log driver duty status, auto-record drive time at 5+ mph, support driver edits with annotations, transfer data to inspectors via email/web service.

Differentiators in ELD experience:
- **Samsara**: Best driver app polish, easiest unidentified-drive resolution
- **Motive**: Easiest co-driver swap, strong personal-conveyance handling
- **Geotab**: Robust ELD core, less polished driver UX

## AI dashcam

| Feature | Samsara | Motive | Geotab |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forward-facing camera | Standard | Standard | Standard (3rd-party SureCam, Lytx integration) |
| Driver-facing camera | Optional | Optional | Optional |
| AI event detection (harsh brake, follow distance, distraction) | Excellent | Excellent | Variable (depends on add-on) |
| Coaching workflow | In-app driver coaching cards | In-app + scorecard | Geotab + 3rd-party UI |
| Video upload (cellular) | Auto-flag → review queue | Auto-flag → review queue | Add-on dependent |
| Driver-side replay (right-to-view) | Yes | Yes | Add-on dependent |

Samsara and Motive AI dashcam are roughly equivalent at this point — both detect distraction, drowsiness, phone use, smoking, following distance, harsh braking, harsh cornering. Geotab requires third-party integration for AI dashcam.

## Driver scoring

| Metric weighting | Samsara | Motive | Geotab |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speeding | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Harsh brake / accel | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Following distance (AI) | Yes | Yes | Add-on |
| Distraction (AI) | Yes | Yes | Add-on |
| Seat belt | Yes | Yes | Yes (engine data) |
| Fuel economy (idle) | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Customizable thresholds | Yes (good) | Yes (good) | Yes (best — fully programmable) |

## Vehicle data depth (Geotab's strength)

Geotab GO9 device captures **richer engine ECM data** — fault codes, fuel level, coolant temp, DPF status, after-treatment fluid, regen state. Useful for:
- Predictive maintenance
- Fault-code alerting before breakdown
- Detailed fuel economy by trip / driver / route

Samsara and Motive cover the basics (odometer, fuel, fault codes) but Geotab goes deeper. For mixed fleets (light vehicles + heavy trucks), Geotab has an edge.

## Integrations

| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| Samsara | Open API, strong TMS integrations (McLeod, TMW, Truckmate), HOS/IFTA exports |
| Motive | API published, growing TMS integrations, native dispatch via Motive Dispatch |
| Geotab | Largest marketplace (SDK + 200+ apps), deepest 3rd-party ecosystem |

Geotab is the platform of choice for carriers needing custom workflows or specialty integrations (refrigerated logistics, hazmat, vocational).

## Pricing structure (typical, 2025)

| Vendor | ELD only | ELD + tracking | + AI dashcam | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Samsara | $30/mo/truck | $35–$45 | $55–$70 | Hardware bundled, multi-year contracts |
| Motive | $25/mo/truck | $30–$40 | $40–$55 | Hardware free with 3-year, $300 outright |
| Geotab | $20/mo/truck | $25–$35 | Add-on dependent | $99–$199 hardware, marketplace adds cost |

Negotiate 3-year vs month-to-month — multi-year contracts save 20–30% but lock you in.

## Driver app quality

Samsara > Motive > Geotab for driver-app UX. Samsara's app feels most polished, fewest driver complaints. Motive close behind. Geotab's driver app (called "Geotab Drive") is functional but less refined.

Driver app matters because driver friction = driver turnover.

## Customer support

| Motive | Self-service strong, phone/chat for paid plans |
| Geotab | Channel partner model — quality varies by reseller |

## Implementation timeline

| Vendor | Typical rollout |
|---|---|
| Samsara | 2–4 weeks for 50-truck fleet, professional services available |
| Motive | 1–2 weeks for 50-truck fleet, self-installable |
| Geotab | 2–4 weeks, reseller-dependent |

## Which fleet should pick which

**Pick Samsara if:**
- Fleet 50+ trucks, professional ops culture
- AI dashcam is mission-critical (heavy insurance pressure)
- Need single-vendor TMS-ELD-camera-tracking
- Willing to pay premium for polish

**Pick Motive if:**
- Fleet 5–100 trucks, growing fast
- Want all-in-one without enterprise pricing
- Drivers will resist a "clunky" app
- Need free hardware on multi-year

**Pick Geotab if:**
- Mixed fleet (light-duty + heavy trucks + vocational)
- Need custom integrations or specialty marketplace apps
- Want most-detailed vehicle data
- Have a channel partner relationship

## Common mistakes

- Selecting ELD based on monthly cost alone — driver-app quality affects turnover
- Bolting on AI dashcam after the fact instead of bundling (cost penalty)
- Letting the ELD provider double as the TMS (lock-in)
- Underestimating driver training time on switch (2–4 weeks of dual-running typical)
- Signing 3-year contracts with no exit clause
- Skipping the driver-facing camera "to save retention" — many insurance carriers now require it

## Where this fits in X3

X3 integrates with Samsara, Motive, and Geotab via official APIs. ELD violations, HOS data, idle %, harsh events, and fault codes flow into the driver and vehicle scorecards. Carriers don't need to pick X3 over their ELD — X3 sits on top.

There is no objectively "best" — the right pick is the platform whose strengths match your fleet's biggest pain point.
