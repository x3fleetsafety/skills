# Smith System vs Other Defensive Driving Programs

Defensive driving programs differ in methodology, content, delivery, and cost. The right choice depends on fleet size, operational profile, and learning preferences.

## Smith System
- 5 keys to safety methodology
- Industry standard for many large fleets
- In-person classroom + on-road instruction
- Certified instructor (Smith-certified or in-house)
- Cost: ~$50-100/driver one-time training; certification typically $500-1500
- Pros: proven methodology, recognized credentials
- Cons: requires Smith-certified instructor, classroom logistics

## CarriersEdge
- Online courses with video + interactive
- DOT-focused curriculum
- Self-paced delivery
- Cost: ~$10-15/driver/month for subscription
- Pros: scalable, low logistic burden, fleet-wide rollout
- Cons: less interactive than instructor-led

## JJ Keller Training (Encompass)
- Comprehensive LMS (200+ courses)
- DOT specifics + general safety
- Self-paced or instructor-led
- Cost: ~$15-25/driver/month
- Pros: largest course library, integrated with JJ Keller compliance tools
- Cons: pricier per driver

## Infinit-i Workforce Solutions
- DOT-focused LMS
- 200+ courses with regular updates
- Per-completion or subscription pricing
- Cost: ~$8-15/driver/month
- Pros: heavy DOT focus
- Cons: smaller library than JJ Keller

## Compass Skills Library (free)
- 100 published FMCSA compliance skills on GitHub
- Carrier can pull SKILL.md files for training reference
- Apache 2.0 licensed
- Cost: free
- Pros: open, customizable, current with FMCSA
- Cons: not turnkey courseware; requires interpretation

## Best fit by fleet size
- **Small fleet (1-15 drivers)**: Compass Skills + targeted in-person quarterly safety meetings
- **Medium fleet (15-100)**: CarriersEdge or Infinit-i subscription + occasional Smith certification for managers
- **Large fleet (100+)**: JJ Keller Encompass + Smith System certified safety director + annual classroom
