# Owner-Operator Random Pool Setup

Owner-operators with their own operating authority must comply with random testing the same as fleet drivers — but cannot run a one-person random pool. Solution: join a Consortium/Third-Party Administrator (C/TPA).

## Why a pool of one doesn't work
Random testing requires that selection be statistically random. A pool of 1 means the driver is either always tested or never tested → not random.

## Consortium membership
A C/TPA pools drivers from many small carriers and selects randomly from the combined pool. The driver gets selected like any other pool member, ensuring true randomness.

## Selecting a C/TPA
Look for:
- DOT-experienced (specifically motor-carrier focus, not just workplace testing)
- Manages collection site network
- Handles MRO coordination
- Reports to Clearinghouse on your behalf
- Reasonable pricing ($8-15/driver/month is typical)

## What the C/TPA does
- Maintains the random pool (typically 200-1000+ drivers across many carriers)
- Generates random selections quarterly
- Notifies you which drivers are selected
- Schedules the tests
- Coordinates collection, MRO review, results
- Reports to Clearinghouse on your behalf
- Maintains your random testing rate records

## Required carrier records (even with C/TPA)
- Pre-employment test result (or query of prior employer)
- Annual MIS report to FMCSA (C/TPA usually files for you)
- Driver random pool history (C/TPA maintains)
- C/TPA contract (carrier-retained)

## Common C/TPAs serving motor carriers
- US DOT Compliance
- DriverFacts
- Drug Test Network
- TIPS Resources
- Health Street (also a testing-network operator)

## Test rates owner-operator must hit
- 50% drug testing rate (controlled substances)
- 10% alcohol testing rate
- Same as fleet — but C/TPA tracks for you across the combined pool

## Audit consideration
At audit, FMCSA wants to see C/TPA membership, pool size proof, and selection records. The C/TPA provides this — keep their reports.

## CFR citations
- **49 CFR § 382.305**
- **49 CFR § 382.111**
